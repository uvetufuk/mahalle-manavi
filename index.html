FAQ

I changed phones and lost my progress

Progress is stored only on the device; there is no cloud backup yet, so it cannot be moved to another phone.

How do I change my ad consent?

In the game under Profile › Ad privacy options (where shown), or on iPhone under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
